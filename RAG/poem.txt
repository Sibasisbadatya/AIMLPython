**The Quiet Code**

In lines of code, a story grows,
A path that only logic knows.
Through screens that softly cast their light,
Ideas awaken in the night.

A single import starts the way,
Like dawn announcing a new day.
Functions dance and classes sing,
Building every little thing.

Errors come, then fade from sight,
As patience turns confusion bright.
Each bug resolved, each lesson learned,
A spark of wisdom gently earned.

So write, create, explore, refine,
One careful line at a time.
For every program, great or small,
Begins with courage most of all.
